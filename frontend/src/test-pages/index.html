Mediterranean Quinoa Salad

A vibrant and nutritious powerhouse packed with protein-rich quinoa, crisp cucumbers, sun-ripened tomatoes, and a zesty lemon-herb vinaigrette.

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1. Rinse quinoa and cook with vegetable broth for 15 minutes.

2. Chop cucumber, tomatoes, olives and herbs.

3. Mix olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper.

4. Combine everything and toss well. Serve chilled or room temperature.
